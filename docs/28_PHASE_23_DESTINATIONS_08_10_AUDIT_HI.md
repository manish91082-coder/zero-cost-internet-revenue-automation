## Destination 8 — Microsoft Agent Store / Copilot agents

### Critical product distinction
Microsoft Agent Store in a customer's Microsoft 365 tenant is an internal discovery/deployment surface. Microsoft's docs describe agents built by an organization's maker, submitted for tenant admin review and published for that organization's users. This is not, by itself, an independent creator payout marketplace.
Independent publishers instead use Microsoft Partner Center / Microsoft Commercial Marketplace, submitting an AI app/agent offer for validation and publication. Microsoft documentation lists AI apps and agents as a publishable offer type.

### Monetization and payout
- Microsoft Marketplace transactable offers allow publisher pricing and Microsoft-managed billing/collection for applicable offer types.
- Standard store service fee: 3% on transactions where Microsoft acts as agent.
- No listing fee to publish offers, according to Microsoft Marketplace FAQ.
- Payouts are monthly; minimum combined payout threshold is $50.
- Payout timing depends on transaction/payment type and maturity; typical monthly payout processing and additional bank-transfer arrival time apply.
- Publisher must accept Microsoft Publisher Agreement and complete validated tax and payout profiles. Payout can be bank or PayPal depending on supported country/payment method; exact India account route must be confirmed in Partner Center before approval.
- Non-enterprise customer orders become eligible after Microsoft receives customer payment; card payments have an additional 30-day escrow period.

### Assessment
- Internal Agent Store: NOT a standalone public creator marketplace or direct payout route.
- Commercial Marketplace AI agent offer: genuine publisher monetization route, conditional on Partner Center publisher enrollment, offer validation, tax profile, payout profile, customer demand and supported technical offer design.
- ₹0 economics: listing fee is documented as zero, but runtime infrastructure, model/API, storage, monitoring, support and taxes can create costs. Therefore zero listing fee does not establish zero-cost fulfilment.
- India payout: not certified in this audit; requires Partner Center country/payment method confirmation.
- Result: CONDITIONAL / HOLD. No publisher account, listing or build initiated.

## Destination 9 — AWS Marketplace (AI agents)

### India seller eligibility and payout
AWS official India seller documentation confirms:
- India-based sellers can sell paid offers to buyers in India.
- Seller needs a standalone AWS India account (not an AWS Organizations linked account).
- Seller registration through AWS Partner Central is required.
- Legal business name, GSTIN/PAN and tax information, seller signature and e-invoicing readiness are part of the India process.
- Indian bank account details (account number, IFSC, account-holder name/address) are required.
- India sellers can receive disbursements only in INR to an India-domiciled bank account; NEFT/RTGS is used.
- Daily or monthly disbursement preferences are available, subject to funds being collected and the applicable settlement schedule.
- India sellers can sell to buyers in India only under the India seller flow; buyers outside India cannot subscribe to those offers.
- AWS Marketplace listing fees apply; fees and 18% GST on those fees are deducted from seller disbursement. Exact current fee amount/rate must be checked in seller terms/Partner Central before any listing.

### Product types and commercial suitability
AWS Marketplace supports SaaS, AMI, containers, machine learning, professional services and AI agent-related offers. Seller eligibility is country-based and India is explicitly included for paid products.
The marketplace route is real, but it is not a zero-cost fulfilment guarantee. A SaaS/agent product can incur compute, model inference, storage, logging, support, security and customer onboarding costs. Listing fees are also deducted from seller proceeds.

### Assessment
- India seller eligibility: VERIFIED conditionally for eligible Indian seller entities meeting AWS requirements.
- Payout: VERIFIED to Indian bank in INR via NEFT/RTGS, after seller onboarding and tax/banking validation.
- Geographic market: India buyers only for the India seller flow.
- Listing economics: non-zero listing fees apply and are deducted from disbursement; current exact fee schedule requires confirmation.
- ₹0 unit economics: FAIL / NOT PROVEN under strict rule.
- Result: HOLD. Commercially real channel, but no listing/build until a zero-cost product model and all five gates are evidenced.

## Destination 10 — Vapi / Retell / Bland partner programs

### Vapi
- Official Solution Partner program is for consultancies, agencies, systems integrators and solution experts implementing voice AI for clients.
- Benefits include dedicated partner manager/support, solution design guidance, co-selling on qualified deals, enablement and partner opportunities.
- Public page does not disclose commission percentage, payout schedule, minimum deal size or guaranteed partner income. These must be agreed in writing.
- Separate Vapi Creators Program advertises up to 15% referral revenue and Vapi minutes/perks, but official page explicitly says applications are temporarily on hold. Do not treat creator signup as currently available.
- Vapi free phone number is a US number and inbound-only; calls and platform usage are not free. Imported international numbers require another provider and outbound requires imported number. This is not an India zero-cost telephony path.

### Retell AI
- Official affiliate program advertises 15% of referred customer revenue for 12 months, 90-day cookie window, monthly payouts and no payout threshold.
- Affiliate payouts are managed by Tolt; India-specific payout onboarding, payment method/fees, tax documentation and bank settlement were not confirmed in the official pages reviewed.
- Retell separately has a Solution Partner program. Public material describes partner referrals, directory listing, co-marketing and support, but does not publish commission rates or fixed partner compensation. Any commission/margin must be agreed in writing.
- Retell voice-agent pricing is usage-based, advertised at $0.07–$0.31/minute before some telephony/add-ons depending on configuration. India telephony can add material cost. Therefore it fails strict ₹0 fulfilment unless a customer advance fully funds costs and settlement timing/risk is controlled.

### Bland AI
- No official public partner/affiliate commission schedule or creator payout terms were verified in this batch.
- Treat any agency/reseller revenue claim as unverified until Bland provides written official terms.
- Do not infer a partner program from generic sales/contact pages or third-party blog posts.

### India telecom compliance gate
TRAI's official sender guidance requires entities sending commercial communication to register as Principal Entity with an access provider and follow relevant registration, consent and preference requirements. Promotional voice calls through auto-dialer/robocall must use the designated 140 series under the 2025 amended framework. Service/transactional robocalls have separate designated-series requirements and sector-specific scope. Do not use ordinary 10-digit mobile numbers for unsolicited/promotional automated calling. The exact use case, caller category, consent basis, number series, telemarketer/access-provider chain and applicable current 2026 amendments must be legally verified before any production call flow.
Voice-agent deployment must also account for customer consent, recording/transcription disclosure, data retention, privacy, opt-out and vendor/subprocessor terms.

### Assessment
- Vapi creator referral: monetization advertised but applications on hold.
- Vapi solution partner: credible co-sell/implementation ecosystem; commission and payout not public.
- Retell affiliate: 15% recurring for 12 months, monthly payouts, no threshold; India payout not verified. This is referral income, not agent rental.
- Retell solution partner: partner channel exists; commercial economics need written negotiation.
- Bland: no public creator payout evidence verified.
- Voice agent fulfilment: usage and telephony are paid; not ₹0 by default.
- India regulatory compliance: substantial gating requirement for outbound/promotional automated calls.
- Result: HOLD for agent rental. Retell affiliate may be separately tracked as referral channel only after India payout and terms verification.
