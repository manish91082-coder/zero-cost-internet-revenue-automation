## Destination 5 — Relevance AI
### Official-source findings
1. Marketplace documentation says agents/tools can be submitted by verified Relevance Builders; the Builder program is currently exclusive. Some marketplace listings are paid and the creator determines listing price.
2. Official affiliate page says affiliates receive recurring commissions for paid referred customers and there is no upfront fee.
3. Affiliate portal currently advertises 15% commission on referred customers' subscriptions for 12 months.
4. Affiliate terms (last updated 2026-01-16) specify:
   - 15% recurring commission for 12 consecutive months on subscription revenue only.
   - Qualified referrals require a new customer, paid upgrade within 60 days, no refund within 30 days, and continued active paid subscription.
   - Commissions become payable 30 days after purchase if no refund; typically paid weekly via Wise, but timing is discretionary.
   - Minimum payout $50.
   - Tax documentation may be required (e.g., W-8BEN); affiliate is responsible for taxes.
   - Paid advertising is prohibited; organic educational content/reviews are permitted with clear affiliate disclosure.
### Assessment
- Agent listing monetization: CONDITIONAL / ACCESS-BLOCKED until verified Builder acceptance and actual creator payout terms are independently confirmed.
- Affiliate: monetization mechanism verified, but requires audience/distribution and active referrals; it is not passive agent-rental income. ₹0 entry appears possible; India Wise receipt, identity/tax onboarding, bank settlement and fees remain unverified.
- Demand: marketplace and paid listings exist, but this does not prove demand for a particular new agent.
- Build/list status: HOLD.

## Destination 7 — Enso
### Official-source findings
1. Enso's current product pages position it as a hosted growth-agent service sold to customers, with a published starting price of $49/month for a single agent surface. This is evidence of Enso selling its own agent service, not of an open creator marketplace.
2. Enso's developer portal documents APIs/MCP and says content/signal API keys are issued per partner. It does not establish a public agent creator revenue-share program.
3. Enso Growth Lab's official bounty page advertises cash payouts:
   - Rumor: $250
   - Signal: $750
   - Proof: $1,500
   - Loophole: $2,500+

Accepted findings carry a 90-day exclusive embargo; Enso gets rights to use, publish and adapt the finding in lab work and client campaigns.
4. Bounty scope excludes ToS-breaking tactics, spam, human-impersonating bots and security vulnerabilities.
### Assessment

- India payout: wire/Wise/PayPal methods are stated, but India-specific onboarding, fees, tax documentation and actual bank settlement are not verified.
- ₹0 economics: no upfront fee stated for bounty submission; research effort and possible tooling/data costs must still be controlled to ₹0. No paid research tools.
- Build/list status: HOLD.
