## Destination 12 — Direct monthly AI-agent rental to local businesses

### Evidence of market context (not proof of our product demand)
India SME Forum's December 2025 report, based on a survey of 7,835 MSMEs during 2024–25, reports:
- 53.8% had adopted at least one digital tool; 46.2% remained fully offline.
- Among digitally active firms, 69% reported at least 10% customer-base growth after digital adoption.
- 52.6% found selecting the right digital tools somewhat or very difficult.
- Advanced ERP, CRM, analytics and cloud adoption remain less developed than basic digital tool adoption.
This is evidence of a broad digitalisation gap and tool-selection difficulty. It does NOT prove demand, willingness to pay, or purchase intent for our proposed AI agent.

### Commercial model logic
Direct customer rental gives the operator control over:
- Specific workflow and customer problem.
- Monthly price and billing date.
- Advance collection / prepaid credit balance.
- Usage caps and fair-use limits.
- Customer data permissions, retention and deletion.
- Support hours and escalation boundaries.
- Cancellation, refund and service-credit rules.

But customer advance alone does not make a product ₹0. The model only satisfies the hard constraint if all of the following are true:
1. Customer funds are received and cleared before any cost-bearing work begins.
2. Advance covers 100% of model inference, hosting, database, messaging, telephony, integrations, tax, payment charges, refunds/chargebacks reserve, human support and incident handling.
3. There is no temporary owner-funded cash gap, including delayed payout, refund or vendor prepayment.
4. The service can run within free-tier quotas and legal data rights, or variable costs are prepaid by the customer with a positive safety buffer.
5. Contract clearly limits scope, usage, liability and service levels; no unsupported guarantee of business outcomes.
6. Customer data is not sent to providers whose terms permit inappropriate training/review of confidential data; provider DPAs and retention terms must be checked.
7. Customer can export/delete its data and the operator can demonstrate access control, audit logs, backup/restore and incident response.
8. Human work is either genuinely automated or explicitly priced and funded by customer advance; it cannot be hidden as free labour.

### Buyer demand evidence status
- Independent market report provides macro context only.
- No customer interview, LOI, deposit, signed order, paid pilot or verified buyer-intent evidence exists in this project at this checkpoint.
- No outreach was initiated.
- Therefore Gate 1 (independent buyer demand) is NOT PASSED.
- No product build or commercial offer is authorized yet.

### ₹0 economics conclusion
Direct rental is architecturally the most controllable route, but a commercial service using paid model/API, cloud, WhatsApp/SMS, phone, integrations or support will not be ₹0 by default. Customer advance can finance variable costs only after cleared funds and a full cost model; it cannot remove platform fees or owner-funded prepayment unless supplier payment timing and customer billing are aligned.
Result: CONDITIONAL RESEARCH CANDIDATE, NOT APPROVED FOR BUILD/SELL.

## Destination 13 — Salesforce AgentExchange

### Partner access and publishing
- Salesforce's Oct 6, 2026 Help article says there are no fees to join the Salesforce Partner Program.
- AgentExchange ISV path requires joining Partner Community and submitting a business plan.
- Salesforce partner material describes business approval, a Partner Application Distribution Agreement (PADA), security review and listing approval before publication.
- Contract can use standard Percentage Net Revenue (PNR) or a fee structure based on projected customer volume, depending on eligibility and commercial arrangement. No universal revenue share should be assumed.
- AgentExchange apps can be offered free or for a fee, subject to Salesforce approval and the applicable agreement.

### Checkout economics and geographic blocker
- Salesforce Trailhead documentation says AgentExchange Checkout is powered by Stripe.
- Checkout is available only to companies based in the US, UK or EU regions. Customers can be from countries supported by Stripe, but that does not remove the seller-company geography restriction.
- Checkout revenue share is 15% of revenue for Checkout transactions.
- Credit-card payments also incur $0.30 per transaction.
- Other monetization / non-PNR models exist and must be defined in the PADA; do not assume Checkout is the only route.
- For an India-based seller entity, the documented Checkout geographic requirement is not met. A separate eligible US/UK/EU entity must not be assumed or created; that would introduce legal, tax, KYC, cost and beneficial ownership obligations outside the current ₹0 mandate.

### Assessment
- Partner enrollment fee: $0 according to current Help article.
- Listing: conditional on business approval, PADA, security review and technical requirements.
- Revenue share: contract-specific; Checkout 15% + $0.30 card fee.
- India payout: Checkout not available to India-based companies under stated seller geography; other contract/payment route not verified.
- Security/data and support obligations: material, not costless.
- Result: HOLD for India-based ₹0 operation.
